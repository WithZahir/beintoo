FAQ

Asked often.

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll answer directly.

Ask on WhatsApp
How long does a brand identity project take?

Most identity projects take 3–6 weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback comes back. You'll get a timeline with clear milestones before any work starts.

Do you work with new and small businesses?

Yes. Several of the brands in our portfolio were built from scratch before opening, including their name story, identity, packaging and launch campaign.

Can I hire you for a single service?

Yes. Every service is available on its own: a logo, a packaging range, a month of social content or a website. Combine them when it makes sense.

What do I receive at the end of an identity project?

Final logo files in every format you need, a brand guidelines document, colour and typography specifications, and the applications agreed in scope, such as stationery, packaging or signage.

Can you help register our trademark?

Yes. Our Brand Registration (IPO) service covers availability search, class guidance and filing support so your name and mark are protected.

How do we get started?

Send us a short brief through the contact form or WhatsApp. We'll ask a few questions, then come back with a scoped proposal. Proposals are free.

Not sure which service you need?

Start with a marketing audit or a short call. We'll recommend the smallest set of services that gets you the result.
