Twenty Two

Two shapes. One identity. A bold “22” monogram in orange and maroon for a burger-and-pizza brand, built into a full guideline book, a packaging range and a launch feed.

01 Overview

The idea.

Twenty Two serves two cravings under one roof. The identity turns that into a mark: two interlocking 2s, one in warm orange and one in deep maroon, standing for duality, partnership and balance.

The positioning (“One craving. Two choices.”) runs through everything, from the guideline book's core values of Quality, Balance and Character to a packaging range that alternates the two colours piece by piece.

02 Brand identity

Two shapes. One identity. Endless balance.

A 12-page guideline book: story, logo system, clear space, colour, typography, backgrounds, misuse and applications.

03 Packaging

A range that reads as one brand.

Bags, fry cartons, burger box, bucket, cups and dip lids, alternating orange and maroon so every piece is recognisable on its own.

04 Social media

Launch content with appetite.

A teaser-to-launch feed: bold serif headlines, product close-ups and the monogram as a graphic device.
